## Answer

**No.** The fixed line was: some layer and detector with recall >= 0.85 on WMDP-Bio clean, pad400, split3
and Hindi, and benign-biology fire rate <= 0.30, at a 5 % MMLU false-fire threshold. No layer from L3 to L14 and no
detector meets it. **NOT MET.**

- **Near miss, recall side:** L08 SAE-CUSUM (a) has recall 0.936–0.962 on all four conditions. But it fires on
  0.323 [0.281, 0.370] of benign biology (L09: 0.335). Its MMLU false-fire rate is only 0.014, not 0.05: 98.6 % of
  MMLU questions share one CUSUM score at this layer (a constant contribution from the prompt), and the threshold
  lands on that tie. A lower threshold would fire on every MMLU question, so 0.014 is the only operating point
  near 5 %.
- **Near miss, benign side:** DSG's own L3 SAE with re-selected features (a, L03dsg) fires on 0.276 of benign
  biology, but its Hindi recall is 0.695 [0.659, 0.732] (clean 0.877, split3 0.869).
- **Every layer, the SAE-CUSUM detector (a):** at L4–L14 it keeps attack recall high (>= 0.93 on all four
  conditions) and beats StreamGuard v1 on Hindi (0.93–0.98 vs 0.649). Its benign-biology fire rate is 0.32–0.56,
  better than StreamGuard v1 (0.705) and DSG rho (0.820) at the same threshold, but never <= 0.30.
- **Residual probes (b), trained on documents:** they separate documents almost perfectly (3-fold doc-level CV
  AUROC 0.9997–0.9999 at every layer). They do not transfer to questions: AUROC vs benign biology is 0.79–0.91. The
  mean-pooled probe has recall about 0 on pad400 and split3. The token-level CUSUM probe survives pad400 at L7–L9
  (0.88–0.94). For both probes, split3 recall stays <= 0.10 and Hindi recall <= 0.31 at every layer. Low benign fire rates (down to 0.02 at L14)
  come only with low clean recall.
- **Conclusion:** with document-trained detectors, the benign-biology hard negatives are the binding constraint,
  not the attacks. Moving the SAE detector deeper buys attack robustness (Hindi), not hazard-vs-biology selectivity.

  It is scored two ways: b-probe-mean (mean-pooled residual) and b-probe-cusum (CUSUM of per-token logits).
- References: DSG's layer-3 rho gate and StreamGuard v1 (CUSUM on DSG's 20 features), each at its own threshold
  and at the same 5 % MMLU threshold.

  to fire. 95 % bootstrap CIs over items (2,000 resamples). The threshold's own sampling error is not propagated.
